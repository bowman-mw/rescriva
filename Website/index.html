Scanner for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Scan the page. Get back a document you can edit.

Rescriva reads print, handwriting and whiteboards, then rebuilds them as Word and PowerPoint files with real headings, equations and diagrams. It opens and marks up the PDFs you already have, too. All of it happens on your device.

Coming soon to the App Store See the screens

For iOS 17 and later. Rebuilding into Word and PowerPoint needs iOS 26.

How it works

  1. 1

    Capture

    Hold your iPhone over a page. Rescriva outlines it, takes it once you're steady, squares it up and lifts the shadows.

  2. 2

    Rebuild

    It reads the page in order, column by column, and finds the headings, lists, tables, equations and diagrams.

  3. 3

    Export

    Save or share it as a Word document, a PowerPoint deck, a searchable PDF or plain text.

Features

Made for real paper, and the PDFs you already have

A camera made for pages

Live page outline, auto-capture, perspective correction and shadow removal. Photograph a book spread and it splits into two pages at the fold.

Whiteboards and handwriting

Whiteboard mode flattens glare and keeps marker colors. Pick Color, Gray, a crisp black and white, or Ink for pen and marker.

Word and PowerPoint, rebuilt

Equations you can edit in Word, diagrams redrawn as shapes, checklists that still check, and notes that become Word comments.

Fix what it isn't sure of

Words Rescriva can't read for certain get a highlighter swipe, like Lineweaver–Bunk. Tap one to fix it before you export.

Your PDFs, too

Open any PDF to read, search, fill in forms and mark up with Apple Pencil. Reorder its pages, or compare two versions to see what changed.

Search every word

Every scan gets an invisible text layer, so search finds the words inside your pages. So does Spotlight.

A library that sorts itself

Folders, smart folders and pins. Receipts, invoices, contracts, recipes and whiteboards get tagged on their own. It all syncs through iCloud.

Share Nearby

Send a file to a computer on the same Wi-Fi. Open share.rescriva.com in its browser and type the PIN. Nothing is uploaded.

Privacy

Your pages stay with you

Read on your device

Reading, rebuilding and exporting all happen on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. There's no Rescriva account and no Rescriva server.

Synced through your iCloud

Your library syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account.

Shared directly
