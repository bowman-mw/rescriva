Support

Something not working?

Rescriva is made by one person. Email comes straight to me, and I read all of it.

Get in touch

support@rescriva.com

It helps to include your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), its system version, the Rescriva version (at the bottom of Settings), and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot helps too. Please don't attach scans of anything private unless I ask for one.

Common questions

  • There's no Word or PowerPoint option. Rebuilding a scan into Word or PowerPoint needs iOS 26 or later. Scanning and searchable PDFs work from iOS 17.
  • A word came out wrong. Words Rescriva isn't sure of get a highlighter swipe. Tap one to pick another reading or type the fix before you export.
  • Glare is hiding the words. On shiny or laminated pages, Rescriva asks you to ease the phone a little to one side and takes a second photo, then merges the two. For whiteboards, choose Whiteboard.
  • My scans aren't on my other device. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Rescriva. PDFs you've opened in Rescriva stay on the device you added them on for now.
  • I want my work out. Export any scan as Word, PowerPoint, PDF, Markdown or plain text, or send it with Share Nearby.
  • I want it all gone. Deleted scans wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything on that device, and your device's iCloud storage settings remove the iCloud copy.

Share Nearby

  • Open share.rescriva.com in the other device's browser. On the same Wi-Fi, your iPhone, iPad or Mac shows up on its own.
  • If it doesn't, check that Rescriva has Local Network access (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network) and that both devices are on the same network.
  • Some work and school networks block the relay. Pair with the QR code on the page instead.
  • Before you type a PIN, check that the number the page shows matches the one in Rescriva.

Reporting a security or privacy problem

If you find anything that contradicts the privacy policy, or a weakness in Share Nearby, please email me with "security" in the subject. I'll put it ahead of everything else.
